# ClausePilot Atelier V5 — design review

## Discarded approach

The older V4 mixed a large marketing hero, a KPI strip and boxed sections, reproducing familiar AI-generated SaaS-dashboard conventions. It also placed source citations far below the narrative.

## Implemented approach

- Document-first typographic hierarchy: the *case* is the page headline, rather than advertising the software.
- A restrained three-zone layout: case library, continuous manuscript, evidence ledger.
- Real clause sources shown adjacent to the interpretation on wide screens; numbered entries rather than repeated feature tiles.
- Data always labeled demonstrative, without misleading AI/model branding or unexplained certainty.
- No generic gradients, ghost charts, pseudo-stats, robot illustrations or unnecessary icons.
- Form state clearly distinguished from analyzed state; stale output cannot be exported.
- Mobile uses a scenario scroller and stacks review sections, without horizontal page overflow.

## Validation

- Nine engine tests passed.
- Contrast/design audit passed on eighteen documented tokens.
- Playwright keyboard-friendly controls, scenario switching, update/stale, abstention and download tested at 320, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1600 pixels.
- No horizontal overflow at tested widths.

This is an editorial product interface adapted to case review, not a facsimile of referenced designer work.
